Put iPhone video on the TV

Cheapest reliable choice: first check whether the television already supports AirPlay—it costs nothing. If it does not, a Roku Express 4K+ is usually the least-expensive dependable AirPlay receiver in Canada and typically costs much less than an Apple TV.

Use AirPlay

You need an Apple TV, an AirPlay-compatible smart TV, or a Roku model that supports AirPlay.

  1. Connect the iPhone and television device to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open a live stream above and start the video.
  3. Tap the AirPlay symbol in the player and choose the television.
  4. If no AirPlay symbol appears, open iPhone Control Centre, tap Screen Mirroring, and choose the television.

Other ways to connect

Can he use Chromecast?

Yes, but iPhone Safari cannot cast every webpage directly. Chromecast works when the video app or player shows a Cast button. AirPlay is usually easier for an iPhone.

What if the TV has no AirPlay?

Add an Apple TV or compatible Roku, or connect the iPhone with the correct Apple HDMI adapter and an HDMI cable. Recent USB-C iPhones need a USB-C-to-HDMI adapter; older Lightning iPhones need a Lightning Digital AV Adapter.
