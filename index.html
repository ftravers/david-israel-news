How to watch a full match

  1. Choose a league above and tap the fixtures link to see the full match list.
  2. Pick the specific game you want. This opens the match page.
  3. On the match page, tap Watch, Live, Replay, or On demand. On DAZN/Screen iL this plays the full match in your browser or app.
  4. For a bigger screen, open the DAZN or Screen iL app (Android, iOS, Apple TV, Roku) and use the same league → match flow. The apps handle AirPlay and screen mirroring automatically.

Questions

Do I need both DAZN and Screen iL?

DAZN covers the four European leagues (Premier League, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1) plus some Israel national-team fixtures. Screen iL covers the Israeli Premier League and Toto Cup. For all five leagues together, you need both. If you only watch Israel, Screen iL alone is enough.

Are there free full matches?

For these five leagues, free full matches in Canada are rare. The four European leagues are officially paid in Canada (DAZN). The Israeli league is officially paid abroad (Screen iL). Free official sites carry highlights and scores, not full games. Some leagues release select matches free occasionally — check the league's official site or the broadcaster's free tier when available.

Can I watch on the TV?

Yes. DAZN and Screen iL both have Android, iOS, Apple TV, and Roku apps. Open the app on the TV device, sign in, and the match plays full-screen. You can also AirPlay from an iPhone to an Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV.
